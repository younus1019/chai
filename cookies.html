02
WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Small Files. Useful Functions.

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser or on your device when you visit. They can help a website remember certain information or recognise a browser during a later visit.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and other tools that store information or help understand how a website is used.

Website Functions

Some technologies help a website remember settings or support its essential features.

Usage Insights

Where analytics tools are enabled, technologies may help measure visits and website performance.

03
WHY WE USE THEM

Supporting a Smoother Experience.

Depending on the website features we use, cookies and similar technologies may help us:

  • Support essential website functions and security.
  • Remember certain preferences where this feature is available.
  • Understand website traffic and performance when analytics are enabled.
  • Help identify technical errors and improve usability.

We should only describe specific cookie purposes as active practices when the corresponding tools are actually implemented on our website.

04
TYPES OF COOKIES

Different Cookies. Different Purposes.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These may support essential functions, security, or features needed to provide a service you request. Where legally permitted, these may operate without consent.

Preference Cookies

These may remember choices such as language or other settings when such functionality is implemented.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics tools are used, these may help us understand visits, navigation patterns, and website performance.

Marketing Cookies

If marketing technologies are implemented, they may be used to measure campaigns or personalise advertising, subject to applicable consent requirements.

This is a description of common cookie categories, not confirmation that Chai Darbar currently uses every category listed here.

05
THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Services Beyond Our Website.

Certain website features may be provided by third parties, such as maps, embedded content, analytics services, social platforms, or other integrations. If enabled, those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies when interacting with their services.

We should identify specific providers in this policy when those providers are actually used and their cookie practices are relevant to visitors.

06
COOKIE DURATION

Temporary or Longer-Term Storage.

Session Cookies

These are generally designed to expire when you close your browser session.

Persistent Cookies

These may remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them, depending on their settings.

Actual storage periods depend on the specific cookie or technology. The relevant periods should be documented for the services used by the live site.

07
MANAGE COOKIES

You Have Control.

Most web browsers allow you to review, block, or delete cookies through their privacy or security settings. The exact steps depend on the browser and device you use.

  • Review cookie and site-data settings in your browser.
  • Delete stored cookies for individual websites or all websites.
  • Block some or all cookies where your browser supports it.

Blocking certain cookies may affect website functions or prevent some preferences from being remembered.

08
YOUR CHOICES

Consent and Your Preferences.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, the website should request that consent before those technologies are activated.

Where a cookie preference tool is provided, you may be able to accept, reject, or customise optional categories. The available choices depend on the controls implemented on the website.

You may also be able to withdraw or change consent later using the provided preference controls. Deleting cookies in your browser may not automatically update every consent record held by a website.

This policy does not itself provide a cookie consent banner or preference manager. Those controls must be implemented separately if required.
