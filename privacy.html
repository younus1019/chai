01
INTRODUCTION

Your Privacy Comes First.

Welcome to Chai Darbar. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or interact with our services.

In this policy, "Chai Darbar," "we," "our," and "us" refer to the Chai Darbar business operating this website. This policy should be read alongside any relevant notices presented when you use particular services.

The information we actually collect depends on the website features and services we use.

02
INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Share With Us.

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive the following types of information.

Contact Information

Your name, email address, telephone number, and other details you choose to provide in an enquiry.

Technical Information

Information such as your browser, device, IP address, or diagnostic data where collected by our website or service providers.

Usage Information

Information about pages visited and interactions with our website, if relevant analytics or logging tools are enabled.

Messages and Enquiries

Messages, feedback, requests, and other details you choose to send us.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through general enquiry forms.

03
HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Used With Care and Purpose.

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions, messages, and enquiries.
  • Provide information about our menu and offerings.
  • Maintain, operate, and improve our website.
  • Help protect against fraud, misuse, and security issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted and, when required, with your consent.

We aim to use information for relevant purposes and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

04
COOKIES AND TECHNOLOGIES

A Better Browsing Experience.

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. Our website or integrated services may use cookies and similar technologies for functionality, preference settings, security, or analytics.

  • Essential technologies may support basic website operation and security.
  • Preference technologies may remember settings where that functionality is implemented.
  • Analytics technologies may help us understand website performance if analytics tools are enabled.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality. Where required, appropriate consent controls should be provided.

05
INFORMATION SHARING

Your Information Is Not Our Product.

We do not treat your personal information as a product to sell. Information may be shared in limited circumstances, including:

  • Service providers: providers helping with hosting, communication, security, or website operations.
  • Legal requirements: when disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • Protection: where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business changes: in connection with a business transfer or restructuring, subject to applicable requirements.
06
DATA SECURITY

Protecting What Matters.

We aim to use reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Responsible Access

Access to information should be limited to authorised people and systems where appropriate.

Reasonable Safeguards

Security measures should be suitable for the information handled and the risks involved.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07
DATA RETENTION

Keeping Information Only as Needed.

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, why it was collected, and applicable requirements. When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete or securely dispose of it where reasonably practicable.

08
YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You Have Choices.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include:

  • Requesting access to certain information about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where applicable law permits.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing where such rights are available.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

09
THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Beyond Our Website.

Our website may link to third-party websites, social media platforms, maps, or other external resources. Those services may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

We do not control external websites and encourage you to review their privacy information before sharing personal information with them.

10
CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Privacy for Young Visitors.

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in circumstances where parental consent or other safeguards are legally required.

If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps where required.

11
POLICY UPDATES

Keeping This Policy Up to Date.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, website features, or legal requirements. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for relevant changes.

12
CONTACT US

Questions? Let's Talk.

If you have questions about this policy or wish to make a request concerning your personal information, contact Chai Darbar using the official contact details provided by your business.
